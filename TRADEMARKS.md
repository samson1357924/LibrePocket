## Reserved names and marks

`LibrePocket`, the LibrePocket logo (once adopted), and the
`dev.librepocket.agent` application identity are reserved by the project.
You may refer to them factually (e.g. "a fork of LibrePocket"), but:

1. **Forks must rebrand.** If you fork this project, ship it under a
   different name, icon, and applicationId. Do not publish a fork to
   Google Play, F-Droid, or any other store under the name LibrePocket.
2. **No endorsement implied.** Do not present a derivative as the official
   LibrePocket app, and do not use the project's marks in a way that
   suggests sponsorship or affiliation.
3. **Store listings.** The official Play listing and the official F-Droid
   metadata are the only authorized distributions under the LibrePocket name.

## Why this exists

Apache-2.0 §6 explicitly withholds trademark rights. This file states the
project's policy plainly so downstream packagers and forks know where the
line is: the code is free, the brand is not.
